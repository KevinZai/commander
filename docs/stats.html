What this data represents

Anonymous, aggregate usage over the past 30 days. We count events but never store your prompts, code, files, or personal context. You can opt out at any time by setting CCC_TELEMETRY=0 before launching Claude Code or editing ~/.commander/config.json.

Privacy

This data is 100% anonymous and aggregate. Read more about what we collect and why on our privacy page.
