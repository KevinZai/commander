# /ccc-content — content templates

Structures the drafter follows in SKILL.md step 2 and step 3. Adapt to the brief; these are skeletons, not word-for-word scripts.

## Blog post

- Hook: a concrete problem or a sourced surprising fact
- Context: why this matters now
- Main sections: H2s with substance, one idea each
- Practical takeaways
- CTA
- Target: 800–2,000 words

## X thread

- Post 1: hook (a specific claim or question)
- Posts 2–8: one concrete point per post
- Post 9: summary
- Post 10: CTA
- Each post ≤ 280 characters

## LinkedIn post

- First two lines carry the hook (they show before "see more")
- Short paragraphs, one idea each
- End with a question or CTA
- ≤ 3,000 characters

## Email campaign

- Subject line: two or three A/B options
- Preview text
- Body: hook → value → proof → CTA
- Unsubscribe footer for any marketing send

## Landing-page copy

- Hero headline + subheadline
- Three value props: feature → benefit → proof
- Social proof section (real quotes only)
- FAQ
- CTA section

## Docs page

- One-sentence purpose
- Prerequisites
- Numbered steps with exact commands
- Expected result and how to verify it
- Troubleshooting

## Tips to pass on when useful

- Paste a URL to repurpose it, for example into five post variants.
- Name the tone ("casual and direct" versus "formal") for a large difference in output.
- Ask for two or three headline options and pick one.
- With `~~social media` connected, a week of posts can be drafted and queued in one session, each confirmed before it is scheduled.
